# PHASE 1 · 论文骨架设计

## 1. 核心 Claim 锁定

### 推荐标题

**EFES: Embodied Free-Energy Self-Models for Self-Diagnosing Vision-and-Language Navigation**

更稳一点的备选：

**Embodied Predictive Self-Models for Self-Diagnosing Vision-and-Language Navigation**

我建议主标题用 **EFES**，副标题里带 **Free-Energy**，因为这会把“意识”压成一个可计算对象，而不是哲学宣言。

### The One Sentence

A frozen VLN-CE policy can be made self-diagnosing and self-correcting by attaching an embodied self-model whose predictive free energy measures departures from the agent’s own sensorimotor manifold, and whose bounded gated residual correction intervenes only when anomaly evidence is high.

### Claim 类型

**方法创新 + 可检验的科学假设**

不是纯工程，也不是纯哲学。更准确地说：

* 方法层：给冻结导航策略外挂一个 self-model
* 科学层：提出并检验一个假设
  **“导航失败首先表现为 agent 自身预测一致性的破裂，而不是外部奖励或显式错误标签的缺失。”**

### 可证伪条件

这篇论文必须允许自己被推翻。最重要的四个证伪条件是：

1. **surprise / free-energy 不能区分 base-policy error**
   * 例如 ROC-AUC 接近 0.5
2. **gate 不具选择性**
   * 要么长期接近 0，要么长期接近 1
3. **bounded correction 不能优于 frozen base policy**
   * 或者轻微提升但明显伤害 easy steps
4. **somatic loop 不带来额外信息**
   * 去掉 `prev_surprise` / `prev_action` / `prev_progress` 后结果几乎不变

如果这四点成立两点以上，这篇论文的中心叙事就不成立。

## 2. 统一理论深度

这里是整篇论文真正的“理论心脏”。
你的原始直觉是“agent 和环境都是物质，意识从耦合中产生”。这个方向可以保留，但在论文里必须降成下面这个更可操作的版本：

### 2.1 操作性定义：什么叫 self-awareness

我们把 self-awareness 定义为：

> **the ability of an embodied agent to internally estimate whether its current sensorimotor trajectory remains on the manifold on which its own policy is predictive, and to modulate action selection accordingly.**

这一定义刻意避开 phenomenology。
我们不讨论主观体验，只讨论**内生的可诊断性**。

### 2.2 统一对象：self-manifold

把 agent 在时间 (t) 的内在可预测区域定义成一个**self-manifold**：

[
\\mathcal{M}\_t := {x : x \\text{ is predictable from } (s\_t, M\_t)}
]

其中：

* (x\_t)：当前 observation feature
* (s\_t)：self-state
* (M\_t)：topological memory / topo bank

直观上：

* 当 agent 还在“自己理解得了”的轨道上时，(x\_t) 应该落在 (\\mathcal M\_t) 附近；
* 当 agent 开始迷路、撞墙、绕圈、选错长期目标时，(x\_t) 会偏离这个 manifold；
* 这种偏离，不需要外部 failure signal，就能由预测失配体现出来。

这就是你论文里“意识从耦合中产生”的数学落点：
**自我不是一个额外 token，而是一个闭环内部的可预测结构。**

### 2.3 最值得写进论文的 4 个命题

## 命题 1：predictive free energy 是 observation surprisal 的上界

设 generative factorization 为

[
p(x\_t, z\_t \\mid h\_t, M\_t)=p(z\_t \\mid h\_t), p(x\_t \\mid z\_t, M\_t)
]

对任意近似后验 (q(z\_t))，有

# [

\\mathcal F\_t(q)

## D\_ {\\mathrm{KL}}(q(z\_t),|,p(z\_t\\mid h\_t))

\\mathbb E\_{q}\\log p(x\_t\\mid z\_t, M\_t)
\\ge
-\\log p(x\_t\\mid h\_t, M\_t).
]

这意味着：**free energy 越大，说明当前 observation 在 agent 自身模型下越“不像话”。**

这就是你 `micro KL + macro NLL` 最正当的理论解释：
它不是随便拼的 loss，而是一个**对当前观测不可解释程度的上界代理**。

### 这条命题在论文里的价值

它让 surprise 从“经验性启发式”变成“有变分解释的 diagnostic quantity”。

---

## 命题 2：在方差上下界存在时，shifted surprise 对偏离 self-manifold 的距离给出下界

如果 macro predictor 使用高斯观测模型：

[
p(x*t \\mid c\_t)=\\mathcal N(\\mu\_t, \\mathrm{diag}(\\sigma\_t^2)),
\\quad
\\sigma\_i \\in [\\sigma*{\\min}, \\sigma\_{\\max}]
]

则其 NLL 满足

[
-\\log p(x*t \\mid c\_t)
\\ge
\\frac{1}{2\\sigma*{\\max}^2}|x\_t-\\mu\_t|\_2^2

* \\frac{d}{2}\\log(2\\pi\\sigma\_{\\min}^2).
  ]

因此对整体 surprise

[
u*t := \\alpha, D*{\\mathrm{KL}} + \\beta, \\mathrm{NLL}
]

做常数平移后，

[
\\tilde u*t
:=
u\_t - \\beta \\frac{d}{2}\\log(2\\pi\\sigma*{\\min}^2)
\\ge
\\frac{\\beta}{2\\sigma\_{\\max}^2}
\\operatorname{dist}(x\_t,\\mathcal M\_t)^2.
]

### 解释

这条式子正是你想要的“下界”。

它告诉我们：
**经过常数平移后的 surprise，不只是一个上界 proxy；它还下界地控制了 observation 偏离 self-manifold 的平方距离。**

这很重要，因为它把“意识信号”从纯统计失配，提升成了**几何偏离量**。

### 这条命题对代码的要求

你当前代码只有 `sigma_min`。
如果想把这条命题写得严谨，代码里必须再加一个 **`sigma_max`**，把 predictor 的标准差 clamp 到 ([\\sigma*{\\min}, \\sigma*{\\max}])。

这就是“论文是蓝图，代码服从论文”的第一处具体升级。

---

## 命题 3：bounded gated correction 保证低 surprise 时非侵入

定义 base policy 为

[
\\pi\_0(a\\mid \\cdot)=\\operatorname{softmax}(\\ell\_t^0(a)),
]

corrected policy 为

[
\\pi(a\\mid \\cdot)
\\propto
\\exp(\\ell\_t^0(a)+r\_t(a)),
\\quad
r\_t(a)=g\_t,\\delta\_t(a),
\\quad
g\_t\\in[0,1].
]

若

[
|\\delta*t|*\\infty \\le B
]

并且 corrected policy 与 base policy 使用**同一 valid action mask**，则有

[
D\_{\\mathrm{KL}}(\\pi ,|, \\pi\_0)\\le 2g\_t B,
]

进一步由 Pinsker 不等式，

[
|\\pi-\\pi*0|*{\\mathrm{TV}}
\\le
\\sqrt{g\_t B}.
]

### 解释

这条命题把你论文里最关键的一句写实了：

> when surprise is low, EFES transparently passes through the base policy.

但要注意，这条命题有两个硬条件：

1. residual 必须有界
   所以代码里最好把 delta 写成
   [
   \\delta*t = B \\tanh(f*\\delta(\\cdot))
   ]
2. corrector 和 base 必须共享同一 valid mask
   不能像你现在那样偷偷把 visited-node mask 塞进 corrected path

否则，这条理论就会被代码自己破坏。

## 命题 4：选择性干预的收益下界

记事件 (E\_t) 表示 “base policy 在时刻 (t) 选错动作”。
设 gate 超过阈值时触发 intervention。记：

* (\\alpha = \\Pr(g\_t>\\tau \\mid E\_t))：对错误状态的命中率
* (\\beta = \\Pr(g\_t>\\tau \\mid \\neg E\_t))：对正确状态的误报率

如果满足：

* 在错误状态上，一旦触发干预，期望单步 CE 改善至少为 (m>0)
* 在正确状态上，一旦触发干预，期望单步 CE 伤害至多为 (c\\ge 0)

那么有

## [

\\mathbb E[\\ell\_t(\\pi\_0)-\\ell\_t(\\pi)]
\\ge
\\Pr(E\_t)\\alpha m

\\Pr(\\neg E\_t)\\beta c.
]

### 解释

这条命题非常有用，因为它直接告诉你：

* 为什么 **surprise ROC / AUC** 是核心分析图；
* 为什么 **gate 要 sparse 且 selective**；
* 为什么你的 `H_critical` 可以正当地定义成：
  “如果 AUC 太低，这个 idea 不值得继续烧卡”。

这条命题不会直接证明整个轨迹 SR/SPL 一定提高，但它给了一个**单步收益下界**。
顶会论文里，这样的理论深度已经很够用了。

## 4. Intro 四段式 Storyline

### 段 1：背景 + 问题

VLN-CE agents must follow language instructions while acting in continuous 3D environments with partial observability, control noise, and no pre-given navigation graph. Topological planners and map-based pre-training have improved long-range planning and cross-modal grounding, yet strong agents still fail in a strikingly silent way: they drift off course, continue executing locally plausible actions, and stop without any internal indication that they are already off track. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07035 "https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07035"))

### 段 2：现有方法的共同缺陷

The common limitation is not merely imperfect perception or planning. Existing methods optimize action selection, map construction, or language reasoning, but they rarely model whether the agent can detect that its own sensorimotor predictions have become unreliable. Recovery is usually driven by heuristics, replanning triggers, or external supervision, rather than an endogenous estimate of “I am no longer behaving on a trajectory that I understand.” LLM-style navigators add explicit language reasoning, but that does not by itself provide step-level diagnosis of embodied mismatch. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11742 "https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11742"))

### 段 3：insight → method

Our insight is that navigation failure first appears as a breakdown of predictive consistency inside the agent’s own sensorimotor loop. If an embodied self-state couples exteroceptive signals with body variables such as previous action, progress, and prior anomaly, then the discrepancy between predicted and realized observations becomes an internal diagnostic signal. We therefore attach to a frozen VLN policy an embodied free-energy self-model with three parts: a self-state module that builds a recurrent self-representation, a predictor that turns predictive mismatch into a free-energy surprise signal, and a bounded gated corrector that adjusts the base policy only when anomaly evidence is high.

### 段 4：贡献

1. We formulate operational self-awareness for VLN-CE as endogenous detection of departures from the agent’s own predictive manifold, and instantiate it with an embodied self-state that couples body and environment signals.
2. We derive a free-energy view of surprise, including a manifold-distance floor under bounded predictive variance and a bounded-intervention guarantee for gated residual correction.
3. We show how this theory leads to a plug-in architecture for frozen VLN policies, together with falsifiable diagnostics based on ROC, gate sparsity, and intervention selectivity.

## Title

**EFES: Embodied Free-Energy Self-Models for Self-Diagnosing Vision-and-Language Navigation**

---

## Abstract

Vision-and-language navigation in continuous environments requires an agent to map language to action under partial observability, control noise, and topological uncertainty. Existing VLN-CE methods improve planning, mapping, or multimodal representation, but they still lack an internal mechanism to determine when their own decisions have become unreliable. We present EFES, an embodied free-energy self-model that augments a frozen navigation policy with a recurrent self-state, a predictive module whose variational discrepancy yields an intrinsic surprise signal, and a bounded gated residual corrector that intervenes only when anomaly evidence is high. On R2R-CE, EFES improves a frozen ETPNav backbone by `[ΔSR]` SR and `[ΔSPL]` SPL, while surprise reaches `[AUC]` ROC-AUC for detecting base-policy error and gate activation remains sparse and selective. These results support an operational view of self-awareness in embodied navigation: an agent need not receive explicit failure supervision to diagnose when it is off-manifold, because predictive inconsistency inside the sensorimotor loop is already informative.
VLN-CE and the main baseline families referenced here are established by R2R-CE, DUET, ETPNav, and BEVBert. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07035 "https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07035"))

---

## 1. Introduction

Vision-and-language navigation in continuous environments (VLN-CE) asks an embodied agent to follow natural-language instructions while acting through low-level controls in a partially observed 3D world. This setting removes several assumptions that simplify graph-based VLN, including a known navigation graph, perfect localization, and oracle transitions. Progress in online topological planning and map-based representation learning has substantially improved long-horizon navigation. Yet the dominant failure mode remains surprisingly simple: the agent drifts away from the intended route, continues to produce locally plausible actions, and stops without any internal indication that its behavior has become unreliable. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07035 "https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07035"))

The central limitation is not only that current agents make mistakes, but that they usually do not know when they are making them. Existing VLN-CE systems focus on better map construction, stronger cross-modal reasoning, larger training data, or explicit language-based planning. These directions improve action quality, but they typically do not endow the agent with an endogenous estimate of whether its current sensorimotor trajectory still lies in a regime that its own policy can reliably predict. Recovery therefore tends to rely on heuristics, replanning triggers, or additional supervision. Even language-heavy agents that generate explicit reasoning do not automatically obtain step-level diagnosis of embodied mismatch. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11742 "https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11742"))

Our starting point is that navigation failure should first appear as a breakdown of predictive consistency inside the agent’s own sensorimotor loop. If the agent maintains a self-state that couples exteroceptive information with body variables such as previous action, previous anomaly, and progress, then mismatch between predicted and realized observations becomes an internal signal of being off-track. Based on this view, we present EFES, an embodied free-energy self-model attached to a frozen VLN policy. EFES contains three parts. A Self-State module forms a recurrent self-representation from body and environment signals. A Predictor measures predictive discrepancy through a variational free-energy-style objective that combines latent disagreement and observation reconstruction. A Corrector applies a bounded gated residual to the base policy’s logits, so intervention grows only when anomaly evidence is high. We use “self-awareness” in this paper in an operational sense: not phenomenology, but the agent’s ability to internally estimate the reliability of its current sensorimotor trajectory.

This paper makes three contributions. First, we formulate operational self-awareness for VLN-CE as endogenous detection of departures from the agent’s own predictive manifold, and we realize it with an embodied self-state that closes the body–environment loop. Second, we derive a theoretical view of the resulting surprise signal: it upper-bounds observation surprisal under a variational model, admits a manifold-distance floor under bounded predictive variance, and yields a bounded-intervention guarantee for gated residual correction. Third, we translate this framework into a plug-in architecture for frozen VLN policies and define falsifiable diagnostics for its success, including error-detection ROC, gate sparsity, and selective intervention gains.

## 2. Method

### 2.1 Problem Formulation

We consider VLN-CE as a partially observable decision process conditioned on an instruction (I). At time (t), the agent receives observation features (x\_t), maintains a topological memory (M\_t), and chooses an action from a valid candidate set (\\mathcal A\_t). A frozen base policy (\\pi\_0) produces action logits (\\ell\_t^0). Our goal is not to retrain this backbone, but to attach a self-model that answers two questions at every step:

1. **Is the current trajectory still predictable under the agent’s own embodied model?**
2. **If not, how should the base action distribution be corrected without unnecessary interference?**

We denote by (b*t) the body state, which contains variables such as previous action (a*{t-1}), previous surprise (u*{t-1}), and previous progress (\\rho*{t-1}). EFES constructs a self-state (s\_t), computes a surprise score (u\_t), and outputs a corrected policy (\\pi).

### 2.2 Overview

EFES consists of three components.

**Self-State.**
A recurrent self-representation couples exteroceptive signals ((x\_t, I, M\_t)) with body variables (b\_t). This state is self-referential because the previous anomaly signal is part of the current body state.

**Predictor.**
A predictive model measures how well the current observation fits the agent’s own internal model. We use a free-energy-style discrepancy with two terms: a latent disagreement term between posterior and prior, and an observation-level negative log-likelihood conditioned on topological memory.

**Corrector.**
A gated residual perturbs the base logits only when surprise is high. The residual is bounded, and the corrected policy shares the same valid action mask as the base policy.

The final action distribution is therefore not a replacement of (\\pi\_0), but a conservative correction of it.

### 2.3 Self-State: Embodied Self-Representation

We define the body vector as

[
b*t = [e(a*{t-1}),; u*{t-1},; \\rho*{t-1}],
]

where (e(a*{t-1})) is an action embedding, (u*{t-1}) is the previous surprise, and (\\rho*{t-1}) is a progress estimate. Let (z*{t-1}) denote the previous latent predictive state. The self-state is constructed by attending from the previous self-state into a set of tokens that includes the instruction, current observation, latent memory, and body variables:

# [

s\_t

f*{\\text{self}}
\\big(
s*{t-1},
{I, x*t, z*{t-1}, b\_t, M\_t}
\\big).
]

Operationally, this means that the agent’s current internal state is not a hidden feature that passively summarizes the environment. It also summarizes the agent’s own recent intervention history. This is the mechanism by which EFES realizes body–environment coupling.

A simple but important consequence follows. Suppose two histories yield the same current observation and the same topological map, but one history arrived there after repeated mismatch and the other did not. Any corrector that conditions only on exteroceptive features must produce the same correction on these two histories. A corrector that conditions on embodied self-state need not do so. The body loop therefore enlarges the class of recoverable behaviors.

### 2.4 Predictor: Free-Energy Surprise

We use a predictive latent state (z\_t) and hidden dynamics (h\_t):

[
h*t = \\mathrm{GRU}([s\_t, e(a*{t-1})], h\_{t-1}).
]

The micro branch defines a prior and posterior:

[
p(z\_t \\mid h\_t), \\qquad q(z\_t \\mid h\_t, x\_t),
]

with latent discrepancy

# [

\\mathcal L\_{\\text{micro}}

D\_{\\mathrm{KL}}!\\big(q(z\_t \\mid h\_t, x\_t),|,p(z\_t \\mid h\_t)\\big).
]

The macro branch retrieves context from topological memory and predicts the observation feature through a Gaussian decoder:

[
c\_t = \\mathrm{Attn}(s\_t, M\_t), \\qquad
p(x\_t \\mid c\_t)=\\mathcal N(\\mu\_t, \\mathrm{diag}(\\sigma\_t^2)).
]

Its reconstruction term is

# [

\\mathcal L\_{\\text{macro}}

-\\log p(x\_t \\mid c\_t).
]

We define surprise as

# [

u\_t

\\alpha \\mathcal L\_{\\text{micro}}

* \\beta \\mathcal L\_{\\text{macro}}.
  ]

This quantity has a clean interpretation.

**Proposition 1 (variational surprisal upper bound).**
Assume a generative factorization (p(x\_t, z\_t \\mid h\_t, M\_t)=p(z\_t \\mid h\_t)p(x\_t \\mid z\_t, M\_t)). Then for any (q(z\_t)),

# [

\\mathcal F\_t(q)

## D\_ {\\mathrm{KL}}(q(z\_t),|,p(z\_t\\mid h\_t))

\\mathbb E\_q \\log p(x\_t\\mid z\_t, M\_t)
\\ge
-\\log p(x\_t \\mid h\_t, M\_t).
]

Thus, high free energy certifies low model evidence for the current observation under the agent’s own predictive model.

Our implementation uses a factorized surrogate of this free-energy quantity, but preserves the same diagnostic semantics.

To connect surprise to geometric deviation, we clamp predictive variance:

[
\\sigma*i \\in [\\sigma*{\\min}, \\sigma\_{\\max}].
]

Let (\\mathcal M\_t) denote the self-manifold implied by the decoder mean (\\mu\_t). Then:

**Proposition 2 (manifold-distance floor).**
For the Gaussian macro term,

[
-\\log p(x*t \\mid c\_t)
\\ge
\\frac{1}{2\\sigma*{\\max}^2}|x\_t-\\mu\_t|\_2^2

* \\frac{d}{2}\\log(2\\pi \\sigma\_{\\min}^2).
  ]

Therefore the shifted surprise

# [

\\tilde u\_t

## u\_t

\\beta \\frac{d}{2}\\log(2\\pi \\sigma\_{\\min}^2)
]

satisfies

[
\\tilde u*t
\\ge
\\frac{\\beta}{2\\sigma*{\\max}^2}
\\operatorname{dist}(x\_t,\\mathcal M\_t)^2.
]

This shows that surprise is not only a heuristic anomaly score. After a constant shift, it lower-bounds squared distance from the current observation to the agent’s own predictive manifold.

### 2.5 Corrector: Conservative Gated Residual Correction

The base policy produces logits (\\ell\_t^0(a)) over the valid action set (\\mathcal A\_t^{\\text{valid}}). EFES predicts a bounded residual

[
\\delta*t(a)=B \\tanh(f*\\delta(s\_t, u\_t, c\_t)\_a)
]

and a scalar gate

[
g\_t = \\sigma(\\eta(u\_t-\\tau)).
]

The corrected logits are

[
\\ell\_t(a)=\\ell\_t^0(a)+g\_t \\delta\_t(a),
\\qquad a\\in \\mathcal A\_t^{\\text{valid}},
]

and

[
\\pi(a\\mid \\cdot)=\\mathrm{softmax}(\\ell\_t(a)).
]

Two details matter.

First, the residual is bounded. Second, the valid action mask is shared with the base policy. These are not implementation conveniences; they are what make the following guarantee possible.

**Proposition 3 (bounded intervention).**
If (|\\delta*t|*\\infty \\le B) and the corrected policy uses the same valid action set as the base policy, then

[
D\_{\\mathrm{KL}}(\\pi ,|, \\pi\_0)\\le 2g\_t B,
]

and

[
|\\pi-\\pi*0|*{\\mathrm{TV}}
\\le
\\sqrt{g\_t B}.
]

Hence, when surprise is low and (g\_t) is small, EFES is provably non-invasive.

This proposition gives the right formalization of “transparent pass-through.” It also explains why the corrected path must not introduce an extra visited-node mask that is absent from the base policy.

Finally, we make precise when selective intervention helps.

Let (E\_t) be the event that the base policy’s action is incorrect. Suppose intervention is triggered when (g\_t>\\tau), with

[
\\alpha=\\Pr(g\_t>\\tau \\mid E\_t),
\\qquad
\\beta=\\Pr(g\_t>\\tau \\mid \\neg E\_t).
]

Assume the expected one-step loss decrease on erroneous states is at least (m>0), and the expected one-step loss increase on correct states is at most (c\\ge 0). Then:

**Proposition 4 (selective-gain lower bound).**

## [

\\mathbb E[\\ell\_t(\\pi\_0)-\\ell\_t(\\pi)]
\\ge
\\Pr(E\_t)\\alpha m

\\Pr(\\neg E\_t)\\beta c.
]

This proposition motivates three central analyses: error-detection ROC, gate sparsity, and intervention selectivity.

### 2.6 Training Objective

We train only EFES while freezing the base navigation backbone. The main loss is

# [

\\mathcal L

\\mathcal L\_{\\text{nav}}

* \\lambda*{\\text{fe}} \\mathcal L*{\\text{fe}}
* \\lambda*{\\text{cal}} \\mathcal L*{\\text{cal}}
* \\lambda*{\\text{safe}} \\mathcal L*{\\text{safe}}.
  ]

The terms are

[
\\mathcal L\_{\\text{nav}} = \\mathrm{CE}(\\pi, y\_t),
]

where (y\_t) is the teacher action,

# [

\\mathcal L\_{\\text{fe}}

\\mathcal L\_{\\text{micro}}

* \\lambda*{\\text{macro}} \\mathcal L*{\\text{macro}},
  ]

# [

\\mathcal L\_{\\text{cal}}

\\mathrm{BCE}(g\_t,; \\mathbf 1[\\arg\\max \\pi\_0 \\neq y\_t]),
]

and optionally

# [

\\mathcal L\_{\\text{safe}}

D\_{\\mathrm{KL}}(\\pi ,|, \\pi\_0).
]

We use the base-policy error label in (\\mathcal L\_{\\text{cal}}) because the gate is intended to estimate when the frozen policy is unreliable. This aligns the learning signal with Proposition 4.

### 3.4 Qualitative and Diagnostic Analysis

We perform four analyses.

First, we treat surprise as an error detector for the frozen base policy and plot ROC-AUC for the binary target (\\mathbf 1[\\arg\\max \\pi\_0 \\neq y\_t]). This directly evaluates the diagnostic premise of EFES.

Second, we examine the distribution of gate activations. A desirable pattern is sparse intervention: low gate mass on routine steps and concentrated high values near failure points.

Third, we visualize trajectories together with surprise and gate curves. In successful episodes, surprise should stay low or return to baseline quickly. In failure-prone episodes, surprise should spike before or at the point where the agent begins to drift, and the correction gate should activate in the same region.

Fourth, we measure teacher-margin change before and after correction. If EFES works as intended, it should increase the teacher-action margin mainly on difficult states while remaining nearly neutral on easy states.

## 4. Related Work

### Vision-and-Language Navigation in Continuous Environments

VLN-CE removes the strong assumptions of discrete navigation graphs and oracle transitions, making planning and control inseparable from partial observability and low-level execution. DUET introduced dual-scale graph reasoning, ETPNav focused on online topological planning with separate control, and BEVBert studied map-based pre-training for spatially grounded navigation. ScaleVLN highlighted the role of data generation and scale in training strong navigators. These lines improve navigation competence, but they do not explicitly model endogenous self-diagnosis of policy failure. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07035 "https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07035"))

### Language-Centric and Memory-Centric Navigation

Another line of work emphasizes explicit linguistic reasoning or long-term memory. NavGPT and NavGPT-2 bring large language models into navigation reasoning, while continual-learning approaches study how VLN agents preserve and update memory across environments. These works broaden reasoning or retention, but they do not directly ask whether the agent can estimate from within its own sensorimotor loop that its present behavior has become unreliable. EFES is complementary: it does not aim to replace planning with language reasoning, but to attach a self-diagnostic layer to a frozen navigator. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16986 "https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16986"))

### World Models and Free-Energy Views of Agency

World models such as DreamerV3, TD-MPC2, and Delta-IRIS learn predictive models to support control or planning. Active inference and the free-energy principle provide a broader framework in which perception and action are tied to minimizing variational free energy. EFES borrows the diagnostic side of this perspective rather than the full action-selection machinery: we use free-energy-style predictive discrepancy as an internal anomaly signal, and combine it with a conservative residual corrector on top of a frozen navigation backbone. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04104 "https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04104"))

---

## 5. Conclusion

We presented EFES, a framework that equips a frozen VLN-CE policy with operational self-awareness. The central idea is that an embodied agent can diagnose its own failure by tracking predictive inconsistency inside the sensorimotor loop, rather than waiting for explicit external failure labels. This view leads to a three-part architecture: a self-state that couples body and environment signals, a predictor that converts mismatch into a free-energy surprise signal, and a bounded gated corrector that preserves the base policy on routine states while intervening on anomalous ones.

The main conceptual finding is that self-awareness in embodied navigation can be formulated without appealing to phenomenology. It is enough that the agent maintain a self-referential state and estimate when current observations deviate from the manifold implied by its own predictive model. Under bounded variance and bounded residual correction, this view yields concrete guarantees on anomaly magnitude and policy deviation.

This work also has limitations. Our theory is local and step-wise; it does not by itself prove sequence-level gains in SR or SPL. The quality of the method depends on whether surprise separates erroneous from routine states. If anomaly detection collapses, selective correction cannot help. A second limitation is that the present formulation inherits the representational capacity of the frozen base navigator.

A natural next step is to move from step-wise diagnosis to temporally extended self-regulation, where the agent not only detects that it is off-manifold, but also selects recovery strategies over multiple steps. Another direction is to test whether embodied predictive self-models transfer across backbones and tasks beyond VLN-CE.
